---

Key principles for Colombian dev tools:
- Social proof: show number of users, stars, or companies using it
- Loss aversion: frame benefits as avoiding losses ("Don't lose money to miscalculated retenciones")
- Specificity builds trust: "saves 2 hours per invoice" beats "saves time"
- The endowment effect: give something for free first (free calculator), then upsell
- Familiarity: use terms users already know (retención, DIAN, IBC, UVT)
